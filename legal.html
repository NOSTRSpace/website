Privacy Policy

Overview

nostr.space ("we", "us") operates this website and the relays at relay.nostr.space and dev-relay.nostr.space. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Website

This website is a static site. We do not use cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics tools. We do not collect, store, or process any personal data through the website itself.

Relays

Our nostr relays accept, store, and forward events as defined by the nostr protocol . Events published to a relay are public by design. Operational logs may contain technical metadata (such as IP addresses and timestamps) for the purpose of abuse prevention and short-term debugging, and are not shared with third parties.
