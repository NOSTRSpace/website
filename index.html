Welcome

A small corner of the nostr universe

Nostr is a simple, open protocol for censorship-resistant communication. It needs relays — small servers that pass notes between people — and we like running them.

No accounts, no tracking, no fees. Just point your nostr client at one of our relays and you're on your way.
